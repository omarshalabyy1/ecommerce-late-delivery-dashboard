## Not applied, and why
- Sparkline or SVG KPI cards (`kpi-cards` T3, `svg-kpi-cards`): 76 px of height, no room for a sparkline; SVG cards are not clickable.
- Bookmark toggles, sliding filter panel, drillthrough, side menu (`interactivity`): the questions fit one page each and the top navigation already works.
- Neumorphism, dark mode toggle, PowerPoint backgrounds (`report-design` T1, T2; `backgrounds-nav`): the backgrounds are drawn by `make_frames.py` and look.md fixes the look.
- Dynamic insight text (`report-design` T13): the header sub-lines are `make_frames.py` visuals; changing them is a frames change, not a tricks change.
- Date picker and preset period slicers (`slicers` T1 to T7): the Year slicer with single select covers the vs-last-year logic.
